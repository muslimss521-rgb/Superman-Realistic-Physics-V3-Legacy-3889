SUPERMAN REALISTIC PHYSICS V4
=============================

Implemented in this build:
- stable camera-directed flight
- real frame-time acceleration/braking
- hover with inertial damping
- boost flight
- upright stable orientation / heading steering
- super speed
- super jump
- invincibility
- heat-vision ray damage
- freeze-breath hold/freeze effect
- super-breath directional force
- super-strength punch impulse + damage/ragdoll
- ground-pound descent + impact shockwave
- grab / hold / throw
- bullet time
- super senses (see-through + night vision)
- centralized F3 menu and direct keyboard controls
- physics helpers: mass, drag, momentum, kinetic energy, impulse, impact energy

Controls:
F3 = menu
F = flight
LShift = hold boost (menu Boost can also lock boost on)
H = heat vision
J = freeze breath
K = super breath
R = super punch
V = ground pound
E = grab / hold
Q = throw
B = bullet time
X = senses
W/S/A/D = flight direction
Space/Ctrl = vertical flight

Notes:
- This version uses only the supplied ScriptHookV SDK and native GTA V calls.
- Costume/PED models, custom animation packs, custom audio and custom particle assets are
  intentionally not embedded because they require user-owned/available assets.
- The powers above are implemented gameplay systems, not menu-only boolean placeholders.
