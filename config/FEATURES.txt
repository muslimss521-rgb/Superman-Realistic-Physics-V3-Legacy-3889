REALISTIC PHYSICS V3
====================

Core:
- mass
- velocity
- acceleration
- momentum
- kinetic energy
- impulse
- aerodynamic drag
- gravity
- impact force
- impact damage

Abilities planned/structured:
- realistic flight / hover / boost
- super speed
- strength / punch impulse
- grab / throw
- ground pound
- heat vision
- freeze breath
- super breath
- bullet-time
- senses
- custom camera
- costume system
- audio system
- configurable controls

The current ZIP is a buildable foundation. GTA-specific native calls, animation assets,
PED switching and particle/audio assets must be integrated with the licensed/available
ScriptHookV SDK and user-owned mod assets.


UNITY ABILITIES PORT
====================
The supplied SupermanAbilities_Unity.zip was reviewed and its gameplay concepts were integrated into the native GTA V project.

Integrated:
1. RealisticSupermanFlight:
   - flyForce 30
   - maxSpeed 50
   - boost multiplier 2.5
   - air drag 1
   - stop drag 5
   - 90% gravity compensation while moving forward
   - visual bank up to 35 degrees
   - smooth bank response 5
   - gravity remains enabled during flight

2. HeatVision:
   - 100 m visual beam
   - physical push force
   - periodic damage

3. FreezeBreath:
   - 15 m range
   - 30 degree cone
   - freezes all peds inside the cone

4. SupermanLanding:
   - landing impact energy uses mass and vertical velocity
   - shockwave radius 20 m
   - falloff-based impulse

5. XRayVision:
   - toggle on Y
   - scans peds up to 80 m
   - pre-render/alpha + markers/lines are used as the GTA-native approximation
