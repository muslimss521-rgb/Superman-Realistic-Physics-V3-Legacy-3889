SUPERMAN CONTROLS

F3     - menu
V      - toggle flight
W/S    - forward/back
A/D    - left/right
SPACE  - up
CTRL   - down
LShift - boost while held
G      - super speed toggle
SPACE  - super jump when on ground
I      - invincibility toggle
H      - heat vision (hold)
J      - freeze breath (hold)
K      - super breath (hold)
X      - ground pound
E      - grab; press again to throw
Q      - throw grabbed target
R      - sonic boom
T      - solar flare
B      - bullet time toggle
Y      - X-Ray vision toggle

All controls can be changed in config/Superman.ini.
Flight uses the vanilla GTA V skydive free_idle pose so the
character does not use the normal running animation while airborne.


Ported from supplied Unity SupermanAbilities package:
- RealisticSupermanFlight.cs: thrust 30, max speed 50, boost x2.5, air drag 1, stop drag 5, lift compensation, and 35-degree bank.
- HeatVision.cs: 100 m beam concept and physical push.
- FreezeBreath.cs: 15 m / 30-degree cone freeze.
- SupermanLanding.cs: impact-energy landing shockwave.
- XRayVision.cs: native GTA approximation using pre-render/alpha/scan markers; GTA has no Unity-style Camera.cullingMask API.
